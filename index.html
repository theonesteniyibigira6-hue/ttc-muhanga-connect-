Built For The Modern TTC Community

Engineered with precision for high-trust communication, academic collaboration, and lifelong professional relationships.

An intelligent, rich social timeline tailored to TTC Muhanga campus life. Share thoughts, multimedia updates, questions, and institutional triumphs.

End-to-end encrypted direct chats, study group channels, and real-time multimedia discussions between peers, faculty, and research cohorts.

3. Official Announcements

Verified circulars from school administration, academic boards, and department heads with authenticated seals and high-priority alerts.

4. Groups and Clubs

Dedicated digital spaces for debate clubs, science societies, sports teams, teacher training guilds, and cultural collectives.

An unbroken bridge between graduating classes. Connect with leaders across education, government, technology, and global academia.

6. Academic Collaboration

Exchange lesson plans, pedagogical methodologies, research papers, syllabus revisions, and teaching practice materials easily.

7. Events and Activities

Interactive campus calendars, graduation symposiums, teacher training workshops, cultural festivals, and alumni homecoming summits.

8. Career Opportunities

Curated job boards, teaching vacancies, university scholarships, internships, and educational fellowships across Rwanda and East Africa.

9. School Memories

A high-definition historical vault preserving campus photo galleries, yearbook chronicles, championship victories, and milestone videos.

10. Digital Learning Community

Integrated virtual micro-workshops, peer tutoring, interactive study halls, and continuous professional development for educators.

11. Real-time Notifications

Instant push alerts via Web PWA & Firebase Cloud Messaging for urgent exam updates, campus notices, group mentions, and event reminders.

Role-verified cryptographic credentials guaranteeing that students, alumni, and certified faculty can trust every interaction on the platform.
